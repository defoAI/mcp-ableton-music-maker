Captures are clips Live recorded into its own project folder through the Capture track. The app plays them in place and never deletes them.

Tokens are estimated from the size of what the tool received and returned, about four characters per token. The model's own context is not visible to the server, so this is the cost each call adds, not a bill.

In words · the six ranges of a mix

The ranges are named the way people talk about a mix, so what you see is what you can say to Claude. Each number is that range’s share of the whole, in dB; 0 dB would be all of it. Nothing here is recorded: the audio is measured in memory and thrown away.
