## Context

What is true that makes this a question. Facts, not argument — and link facts rather than
restating them.

## Options

- **A. <name>** — the case for, then the case against.
- **B. <name>** — same.
- **C. <name>** — including options being deliberately refused, and why.

## Recommendation

One option, with the reasoning compressed to its load-bearing sentence. A recommendation
that hedges is not one.

## Cheapest available evidence

What would settle this fastest, if evidence is wanted before deciding.

## Decision

*Filled in when made.* What was chosen, on what date, by whom, and why — including what the
decision gave up.

## Consequences

What changes downstream: documents to rewrite, tests to add, issues to open or close, work
that becomes moot.

---

*Never delete the options not taken. The reasoning is the value, and the question tends to
come back.*
